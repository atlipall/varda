Get overlays from ogc.gis.is

Iceland’s public map server (ogc.gis.is, run by Náttúrufræðistofnun for about 40 agencies) hands out 507 layers as Shapefile zips, and lets the browser download them directly (no helper, no cost). The Overlays tab gets a way to list and download them; what you download is an ordinary overlay, kept on this device and working offline.

Your choices: a picked list of travel layers in plain words plus a search over all 507; Update in its ⓘ, nothing automatic.

Mockup v1, 19 Nov 2026 · iPhone screens at 390 × 844 · the head unit gets the same screens at car size

The picked list

Huts and stops: Mountain huts; Huts and fishing lodges; 4x4 radio repeaters (Ferðaklúbburinn 4x4) · Rest stops; Places to visit; Saga sites; Film locations (Ferðamálastofa).
Driving and hiking: No-driving zones; Hiking trails; National park boundary (Vatnajökulsþjóðgarður) · Þjóðlendur and afréttir (Óbyggðanefnd).
Hazards: Glacier crevasses 2025 (Veðurstofan) · Reykjanes eruption hazard zones; Walking routes to the eruption area (Náttúrufræðistofnun) · Drone no-fly zones (Samgöngustofa).
Coverage: 4G and 5G mobile coverage (Fjarskiptastofa).
Nature: Hot springs; Protected waterfalls; Caves with guided tours; Important bird areas; Natural birch woods (Náttúrufræðistofnun, Land og skógur).
