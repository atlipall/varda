Maps and overlays in one panel

Today overlays have their own panel (⋯ → Overlays) and maps are reached three ways (the map button, ⋯ → Maps, Settings’ map row). This puts the overlays in the Maps panel, on their own tab (Maps | Overlays) and takes Maps and Overlays out of the ⋯ menu. The map button opens the panel; Settings’ map row still does too.

Your choices: an overlay is shown or hidden at once (Apply stays for the map and hillshading only); one Add… takes .img, .hgt and .kml/.kmz and puts each file in its section; Settings keeps its link.
