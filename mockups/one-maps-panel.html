One Maps panel

Today there are two: the map button opens a short Map picker (switch map, hillshading, “Manage maps…”), and ⋯ → Maps opens the Maps panel (radio + chevron rows, add, download, elevation, storage). Both list the same maps in different ways. This puts them into one Maps panel. The map button, ⋯ → Maps and Settings’ map row all open it.

Changes, following Apple’s guidelines and your notes. One way to pick: tap a row to tick it, as in iOS Settings lists, and adding a map ticks it too. Hillshading works the same way, and tapping the ticked elevation files again turns hillshading off. Apply closes the panel and shows the ticked map; ✕ changes nothing. ⓘ opens a map’s or elevation files’ details (as in Wi‑Fi settings). Hillshading has a None row. Apply is dimmed until something changes. The map button is always on the map. Every target is at least 44 pt.
